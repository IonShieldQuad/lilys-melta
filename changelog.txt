Version: 0.5.0
Date: 2026-10-25
  Graphics:
    - The firing beam is no longer a hairline. rendering.draw_line's `width` is in PIXELS
      (32 per tile) and the old layer table was written as if it were tiles, so the whole
      four-line stack was at most 1.90 px = 0.06 tiles wide -- thinner than the barrel it
      came out of. Measured from a 64 px/tile screenshot, the lance is now 1.03 tiles wide
      at half maximum (16.5x wider), puts 2.8x the light into the scene, and the peak is
      no brighter (so it gained body, not glare)
    - The beam is built in layers now: two sharp core lines, three additive glow sprites
      (wide soft haze -> tight bright gas), six boiling sheath tongues that re-roll a few
      times a second, a muzzle flare plus a directional blast cone, an impact flare that
      follows the body it is cutting, and a tip flare where the lance runs out of reach
    - The glow reads on dark ground: the sprite layers blend additively, and their
      textures are generated white with the shape in the alpha channel
      (graphics/beam/generate.py), so one set of five textures is tinted with whatever
      fuel is burning (oil orange, gas blue, melta green, napalm red)
    - The beam fades out over 10 ticks instead of snapping off, and it keeps fading when
      the turret or its target dies mid-beam: a dead turret's lance is handed to a fade
      list, drawn from its last geometry and then freed, so no beam can be left stuck in
      the air
    - Cheaper than the old beam despite owning 17 render objects per turret instead of 15:
      the draw path allocates nothing per tick (measured -15% script time with six turrets
      firing continuously)
  Fixed:
    - The impact flare no longer lingers on a dead body: it is re-positioned to its target
      every tick and hidden the instant that body dies
    - The core lines are mixed toward white; a fuel-coloured line over an additive glow
      measured darker than its surroundings and drew a dark seam down the middle of the
      beam
    - Beams no longer outlive their turret's death: mining or destroying a firing melta
      used to leave the render objects destroyed mid-fade, now the lance is retired and
      freed through a fenced path
    - The inspect panel now prints the damage the turret actually deals. The beam is
      script damage, not the engine's, so the panel had no damage line at all; the text
      is generated from constants.lua (14 per pulse cold, 56 at full charge, 85 % fire +
      15 % physical, 7.5 pulses/s, 82 % pierce falloff up to 12 bodies, every fuel
      multiplier, the asteroid channel) and is translated in locale/en and locale/ru,
      so the tooltip cannot drift from the numbers control.lua applies. Verified by
      measuring a pulse against an unarmoured dummy: 56.00 hp at full charge on light
      oil, and 89.63 = 56 x 1.60 on enriched melta fuel

Version: 0.4.0
Date: 2026-10-25
  Graphics:
    - The turret is modelled and rendered in Blender now instead of drawn: a rusted
      industrial emplacement plus a wide-bore melta cannon (chunky breech, ribbed copper
      heat coils, vented shroud, welded seams, rivets, dents, chipped paint, rust streaks)
      rendered for all 64 directions off one fixed top-down key light
    - The cannon is ~2.4x longer and ~2x fatter than the old drawn head, so it reads as a
      real gun next to the vanilla gun and laser turrets
    - The muzzle's front face is 1.512 tiles from the pivot (it was 0.66): the beam starts
      at the barrel mouth, and gun_barrel_length / the beam anchors follow the art
    - The hull art is the mod's own render, so the turret looks the same with or without
      the Space Age rocket turret installed
    - selection_box widened to +-1.6 tiles and drawing_box_vertical_extension added so the
      big cannon is fully clickable and does not clip behind neighbours; collision_box is
      unchanged at +-1.2 tiles, so existing blueprints place exactly as before
    - The turret, the item that places it, the technology and thumbnail.png use icons
      rendered from that same model now, so no vanilla turret icon art is borrowed
    - The fluid box became a symmetric four-port set at 1.12 tiles carrying vanilla
      pipe covers, and the hull is de-squashed in Y so the north and south sockets
      meet it: a pipe ends in a visible socket on all four sides
  Fixed:
    - The beam no longer appears while the turret is still turning: it waits until the
      head is within one rotation step (2.16 deg) of the target, and the muzzle plays a
      short charge-up glow instead. dps is unchanged against a target the barrel is
      already on (measured: 673.0 dps before and after)
    - The beam now starts exactly at the barrel mouth in every direction, instead of at a
      fixed point in front of the pivot: the rendered head is a pitched-camera image, so
      the mouth sits 1.51 tiles from the pivot pointing north but only 0.39 pointing south
      (a raised muzzle is drawn further up the screen)
    - Damage is fire with a physical slice instead of a damage type of the mod's own:
      each pulse is 85 % fire + 15 % physical out of the same total, and the `thermal`
      damage type is gone from the mod entirely
    - The firing sound is ~15 dB louder in the mix: the loop was re-mastered
      (-26.4 -> -15.2 dBFS RMS) and now sits at -18.3 dBFS against the vanilla
      flamethrower turret's -19.2, with aggregation capped at 2 voices so several
      turrets cannot clip
  Notes:
    - Art pipeline lives in graphics/_blender/ (melta_render.py, assemble.py,
      check_sheets.py, muzzle_marker.py, check_beam_anchor.py, README.md); re-render with
      one command, no hand-drawn pixels
    - muzzle_marker.py projects the model's own muzzle points through Blender's camera and
      check_beam_anchor.py tests the result against every shipped frame, so the beam anchor
      can be re-derived after any re-render instead of guessed
    - The 15 % physical slice is absorbed by the biters' flat physical armour: dps is
      unchanged against unarmoured targets but ~8-15 % lower against medium, big and
      behemoth biters. C.physical_share is the dial if that trade is not wanted
    - Pipe joins: east, south and west meet the hull; north leaves ~0.12 tiles, and
      vanilla assembling-machine-2 (same +-1.2 collision box) leaves 0.22 on its own
      north side. Measured by graphics/_blender/pipe_sockets.py --check

Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-10-22
  Features:
    - Own sound set, synthesised (sound/_generator/melta_sounds.py): a jet-exhaust loop with a
      sizzling top layer, plus pressure build-up and shut-down cues
    - Fuel defines the beam: light-oil (orange, 1.0x), petroleum-gas (blue, 0.85x),
      enriched melta-fuel (green-white, 1.6x), incendiaries napalm (deep red, 1.35x)
    - Enriched melta-fuel is now laced with uranium: 20 light oil + 2 uranium-238 + 1 sulfur -> 100
    - Burn rate raised to ~19/s cold and ~47/s held at full intensity (was ~1/s)
  Bugfixes:
    - The fuel surcharge could throw "Fluid index out of bounds": remove_fluid is (index, amount)

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-10-22
  Changes:
    - Fuel is now a liquid: melta-fuel, brewed from light oil, piped straight into the turret
      (the turret is a fluid turret for that reason; its stream is invisible and only carries
      the fluid burn, the beam is drawn by the script)
    - New drawn 64-direction emitter head replaces the recoloured rocket pod; the hull art is
      still the vanilla rocket turret, untouched
    - The beam now runs its full length instead of stopping at the first body it cuts
    - Beam is layered (haze / glow / hot / white core) with a boiling plasma sheath on the
      outer edge, a muzzle flash and a burn pop on every body it cuts
    - Friendly forces are never damaged; the beam passes over your own walls like any turret
  Bugfixes:
    - A turret with no fuel no longer damages anything
